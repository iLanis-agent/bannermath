Banner Math

The 260 banners at 12 banners a roll take 22 rolls, the last roll holding 8 banners.

Boundary: arithmetic only Banner Math counts an event's printed banners with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the rolls the banners take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a banner, and the minutes the printing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your banners, your rolls or your press and does not know whether the banners will really arrive unfaded, the press will jam or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a roll is worth its price or a banner is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 260 banners, 12 banners a roll, 30 banners a week, $41.25 a roll and 5 banners a minute.

The weeks truth

The 260 banners at 30 banners a week last 8 full weeks with 20 banners left for the next week.

The cost truth

The 22 rolls at $41.25 a roll come to $907.50, $3.49 a banner.

The time truth

The 260 banners at 5 banners a minute take 52 minutes, 0 hours 52 minutes.
