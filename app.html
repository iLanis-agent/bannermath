Banner Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's printed banners with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the rolls the banners take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a banner, and the minutes the printing takes. It uses no named constants. The banners, the roll size, the weekly use, the roll price and the printing speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your banners, your rolls or your press and does not know whether the banners will really arrive unfaded, the press will jam or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a roll is worth its price or a banner is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The rolls truth

Rolls: the banners divided by the banners a roll, rounded up, with what the last roll holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the banners divided by the banners used a week, with the banners left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the rolls times the roll price, to the cent, and spread over the banners.

The time truth

Time: the banners divided by the banners printed a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Banner Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
